Guide · How this site was made

An engine room tour.

Here is how this site was made: the design system, the motion, and the tech. A portfolio about building things ought to show its own blueprints.

Concept

The spine of the site is one hand-written WebGL particle engine. No Three.js, no libraries. It renders about 14,000 points that reshape themselves seven times as you scroll: a knot, a browser window, a bar chart, a lattice, chat bubbles, a network, and a galaxy.

That is the pitch in a single image. The same intelligence, reshaped for whatever job the business needs done.

Palette

Iron-gall ink and gold leaf, the palette of an old bound book. The ground is a warm soft-black (never pure), the text is ivory, the accent is antique gold, and a moonlit slate is reserved almost exclusively for the particles.

Typography

Three faces with real pedigree, none of them fashionable defaults: a Goudy Oldstyle revival (1915) for display, a humanist sans for the argument, and a typewriter face for the archival voice.

Intelligence, installed.Sorts Mill Goudy · display
Body copy carries the argument in a warm humanist sans, sized for a 65-character measure.Alegreya Sans · body
Catalog codes · labels · dataCourier Prime · utility

All three faces are subset to Latin and embedded as base64, so the page makes zero external requests.

Motion system

  • Particle morphs are scroll-driven: each section declares a target form; transitions ease over ~1.2s with per-particle stagger.
  • Headlines reveal through line masks, and the supporting copy follows a beat later.
  • Ambient drift, mouse parallax and depth-attenuated point sizes keep the field alive between morphs.
  • Everything honors prefers-reduced-motion. Reveals become instant and the field holds still.

Engineering notes

  • One self-contained HTML file, with the fonts, styles, and engine all inlined. No CDNs and nothing to install.
  • Custom GLSL vertex/fragment shaders; positions blended on-GPU, buffers swapped CPU-side at section boundaries to avoid pops.
  • Particle count adapts to device (≈14k desktop / ≈7k mobile); DPR capped at 2 for battery sanity.
  • A small client-side router serves this guide at #/guide, so the link survives a refresh on static hosting.
