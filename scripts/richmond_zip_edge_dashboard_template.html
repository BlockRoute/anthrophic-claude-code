Richmond ZIP Edge: one metro, 74 different markets

The metro headline says "moderate seller's market, 99% of asking." The ZIP-level data says that's an average of extremes: some ZIPs clear in 5 days at 109% of ask while others sit 90+ days below asking. This dashboard scores every ZIP in the Richmond metro so you know which market you're actually standing in — before the other agent does.

1 · Market Heat Index leaderboard

Every ZIP scored 0–100 on four demand signals — days on market, sale-to-list ratio, share sold above list, and share off-market within two weeks — each taken as a percentile rank against the rest of the metro, then averaged (3-month rolling data so one weird month can't fake a trend). 100 = the hottest market in the metro. Showing ZIPs averaging 10+ sales/month; the full table below has all of them.

2 · The opportunity map

Heat (vertical) against 5-year appreciation vs. the metro median (horizontal). Upper-left is the money quadrant: buyer demand is hot today but the ZIP hasn't yet appreciated like the rest of the metro — that's where equity catch-up tends to happen.

3 · Momentum: who's heating, who's cooling

What you're looking at & how it was built

Source. Redfin's public Data Center feed (zip_code_market_tracker and county_market_tracker) — monthly, ZIP-level closed-sale statistics for every ZIP in the Richmond, VA metro, 2012 to present. It's free and public, but it ships as a ~1 GB national raw file with no per-ZIP view, which is why effectively no working agent uses it.

  1. Extract: streamed the national file, kept Virginia rows, restricted to parent metro = Richmond, VA, property type "All Residential."
  2. Smooth: 3-month rolling averages per ZIP, and ZIPs under ~3 sales/month excluded from scoring (under 10/month flagged thin).
  3. Score: Heat Index = mean percentile rank across the four demand signals. Momentum = Heat now minus Heat a year ago. Value gap = ZIP 5-yr appreciation minus the metro median (__APPR__%).
  4. Flag: Heat ≥ 80 and rising → Listing Goldmine; Heat ≥ 70 with appreciation below metro → Catch-up Buy; Momentum ≤ −15 → Buyer Leverage.
Honest caveats: data runs through __ASOF__ (Redfin's public file lags a few months). Median prices in small ZIPs swing with the mix of what happened to sell. The Heat Index ranks ZIPs against each other within this metro — it is not a forecast, an appraisal, or a guarantee. Verify against fresh MLS comps before you put a number in front of a client.
